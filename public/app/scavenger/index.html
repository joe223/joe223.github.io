Scavenger

Understand your local development storage

0.1.0 · 2026-10-21

Scavenger is a native macOS app for examining development dependencies, shared package caches and supported AI tools’ local session records. Analysis runs locally and starts only when you choose to begin. Selected, supported items can be moved to the Trash after separate confirmation and authorization. Scavenger is currently in development; this page does not announce App Store availability.
